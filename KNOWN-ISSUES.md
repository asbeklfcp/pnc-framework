# ⚠️ PNC Framework v0.1 — Known Gameplay Issues

This is the first public release for Post Nut Calamity 0.4.2, Windows x64. Several modified enemy encounters have known problems; this build is not fully stable or bug-free.

Currently reported issues:
- Baphomet may fail to pursue, attack effectively, or teleport consistently.
- Wolf's second-form grab animation may occasionally fail.
- Wendigo may retain a red damage tint and behave inconsistently after defeat.
- Dragon may also exhibit lingering damage-tint behavior.
- Certain high-heat situations may prevent expected enemy captures.

These reports may depend on encounter conditions. Other bugs may exist. Future updates will focus on correcting these problems.

For reports, include the gameplay profile, enemy, reproduction steps and relevant BepInEx/LogOutput.log excerpts. Remove personal paths or other private information before posting logs.

Other limitations: native-font fallback; Beast's enlarged yellow capture layer; incomplete physical-controller and Edi device coverage. Custom action bindings do not replace every native gameplay control. Optional Joker/Witch packages, experimental Archive Passage and creator tools are not bundled.

The updater interface and private update workflow were tested. Automatic delivery from the public GitHub host has not been verified for this first release. Download ZIPs manually from GitHub Releases. No private server or personal PC is required.
